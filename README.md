# Peng JI / 季鹏

[中文](README.zh-CN.md)

**PhD researcher · AI for Science · Harbin Institute of Technology**

I am a PhD student in the School of Astronautics at Harbin Institute of Technology. I study how sensor signals, physics, and simulation can support damage assessment and protective design for spacecraft. My work connects scientific machine learning with simulation pipelines and auditable research agents, with an emphasis on useful predictions, traceable evidence, and reliable scientific software.

[Personal website](https://guhou-hvi.github.io/) · [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=qJH4JBcAAAAJ) · [jipeng@stu.hit.edu.cn](mailto:jipeng@stu.hit.edu.cn)

## Selected projects

- **[Whipple RPP](https://github.com/guhou-hvi/whipple-rpp-benchmark)** — Predicting the protection remaining in a damaged Whipple shield from acoustic-emission signals. Ballistic-limit equations define spatial targets, while model comparisons examine global reconstruction, weak regions, and extrapolation.
- **[AE-PDSM](https://github.com/guhou-hvi/ae-pdsm)** — Mapping acoustic-emission signals to perforation state and two-dimensional pitting-damage morphology, connecting in-situ sensing with quantitative spacecraft damage assessment.
- **[elsevier-figure-style](https://github.com/guhou-hvi/elsevier-figure-style)** — An independent, unofficial research-figure toolkit and Agent skill that turns published figure requirements into reusable plotting styles, checks, and visual review workflows.
